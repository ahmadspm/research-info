Grants & Research Funding

I have led and contributed to competitive research grants across Edith Cowan University, the Cyber Security Cooperative Research Centre, CSIRO Data61, and national research initiatives, contributing to research funding exceeding half a million AUD. My funded research focuses on trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, Digital Twins, critical infrastructure resilience, advanced manufacturing, and cyber-physical systems.

Competitive Research Grants

CSIRO–AEMO AR-PST Stage 5 Grant — Co-Lead & Chief Investigator

Project: Securing DER Stability via a Trusted Sovereign Monitor for EV Charging Systems.

Organisation: Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) and Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO).

Duration: 2025–2026
Funding Secured: AUD $163,367

This project develops a Trusted Sovereign Monitor architecture for securing Distributed Energy Resources (DER) and EV charging infrastructure through trusted firmware validation, cyber-physical resilience, and secure energy transition technologies.

ECU Early and Mid-Career Researcher (EMCR) Grant — Stream 2

Role: Principal Investigator

Project: Towards Resilient Factories: Digital Twins for Safeguarding Manufacturing Systems.

Organisation: Edith Cowan University EMCR Grant Scheme.

Duration: 2026–2027
Funding Secured: AUD $35,489

The project investigates Digital Twin-driven cybersecurity approaches for Industry 5.0 manufacturing environments, integrating AI, knowledge graphs, cyber-physical security, and resilient industrial automation.
