Training & Workshops

I develop technical briefings, scenario-based workshops, research demonstrations, and guided exercises for academic, industry, and government teams. Sessions can be scoped around an organization's infrastructure, research question, and participants' experience.

Discuss a workshopSee past engagements

Training formats

Options range from a focused guest lecture or technical briefing to an interactive half-day or full-day workshop, cyber range exercise, or multi-session research program. Content, access to specialist facilities, timing, and delivery arrangements are agreed for each engagement.

Topics available for tailored sessions

OT and critical infrastructure response

Use an industrial scenario to work through threat identification, MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, incident decisions, recovery priorities, and cyber-physical consequences.

Suitable for: infrastructure operators, security teams, and applied researchers.

Cyber ranges and wargaming

Explore Red and Blue Team workflows, attack paths, security monitoring, and structured after-action reflection in an isolated testbed or tabletop setting.

Suitable for: security teams, students, and government or industry stakeholders.

Secure digital twins and AI

Connect systems modeling and simulation to threat modeling, testbed evidence, resilience analysis, and human oversight of AI-assisted decisions.

Suitable for: engineering, energy, manufacturing, and research teams.

LLMs, AI agents, and secure software

Examine LLM-generated code, evaluation methods, AI agent boundaries, cybersecurity knowledge graphs, and risks in AI-assisted security operations.

Suitable for: software teams, researchers, and curriculum groups.

Research methods and HDR development

Work through research question design, experimental plans, reproducible evidence, publication development, and candidature milestones.

Suitable for: research students and interdisciplinary project teams.
