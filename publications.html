Selected Publications

Selected research contributions in trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, Digital Twins, Industry 5.0, cyber-physical systems, and secure software engineering.

A Unified Framework for Human–AI Collaboration in Security Operations Centers with Trusted Autonomy

Authors: Ahmad Mohsin, Helge Janicke, Ahmed Ibrahim, Iqbal H. Sarker, Seyit Camtepe.

Venue: ACM Transactions on Internet Technology, 2026.

This work proposes a trusted autonomy framework for Security Operations Centres by integrating AI autonomy levels, human oversight, and trust calibration mechanisms to support effective human–AI cyber defence collaboration.

DOI ACM Publisher

BRIDG-ICS: AI-grounded Knowledge Graphs for Intelligent Threat Analytics in Industry 5.0 Cyber-Physical Systems

Authors: Padmeswari Nandiya, Ahmad Mohsin, Ahmed Ibrahim, Iqbal H. Sarker, Helge Janicke.

Venue: Cybersecurity, Springer Nature, 2026, 9, Article 167.

This research introduces an AI-driven knowledge graph framework integrating industrial assets, vulnerabilities, and adversarial behaviours to enable context-aware threat reasoning and cyber resilience assessment in Industry 5.0 environments.

DOI Springer Publisher

CyberAlly: Leveraging LLMs and Knowledge Graphs to Empower Cyber Defenders

Authors: Minjune Kim, Jeff Wang, Kristen Moore, Diksha Goel, Derui Wang, Ahmad Mohsin, Ahmed Ibrahim, Robin Doss, Seyit Camtepe, Helge Janicke.

Venue: Companion Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2025, pp. 2851–2854.

CyberAlly presents an LLM and knowledge graph enhanced cyber defence assistant that supports Blue Teams through incident analysis, alert understanding, and mitigation recommendations within cyber range environments.

DOI Open Access

Explainable AI for Cybersecurity Automation, Intelligence and Trustworthiness in Digital Twin: Methods, Taxonomy, Challenges and Prospects

Authors: Iqbal H. Sarker, Helge Janicke, Ahmad Mohsin, Asif Gill, Leandros Maglaras.

Venue: ICT Express, Elsevier, 2024, Volume 10(4), Pages 935–958.

This survey analyses explainable AI methods for cybersecurity Digital Twins, highlighting approaches for automation, intelligent decision-making, transparency, and trustworthy cyber-physical security operations.

DOI Elsevier

Can We Trust Large Language Models Generated Code? A Framework for In-Context Learning, Security Patterns, and Code Evaluations Across Diverse LLMs

Authors: Ahmad Mohsin, Helge Janicke, Adrian Wood, Iqbal H. Sarker, Leandros Maglaras, Naeem Janjua.

Venue: arXiv preprint arXiv:2406.12513, 2024.

This research investigates secure code generation using Large Language Models through security-aware In-Context Learning patterns, evaluation frameworks, vulnerability analysis, and secure software engineering practices.

Paper

Digital Twins and the Future of their Use Enabling Shift Left and Shift Right Cybersecurity Operations

Authors: Ahmad Mohsin, Helge Janicke, Surya Nepal, David Holmes.

Venue: IEEE International Conference on Trust, Privacy and Security in Intelligent Systems and Applications (TPS-ISA), 2023.

This work explores how Digital Twins can support proactive and continuous cybersecurity operations by enabling security testing, monitoring, and validation throughout cyber-physical system lifecycles.
