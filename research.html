Research Focus Areas

My research develops trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, and Digital Twin solutions for resilient cyber-physical systems, critical infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, and future energy systems. The research integrates artificial intelligence, security engineering, knowledge graphs, and human-centred approaches to address emerging challenges in digital transformation.

Critical Infrastructure Resilience

My research develops secure and resilient cyber-physical systems for critical infrastructure, including maritime ports, industrial control systems, and operational technology environments. Through ECU’s ACDC program, I contributed to cyber range development, IT/OT security architectures, adversary emulation scenarios, and Security Operations Centre capabilities. I have also contributed to critical infrastructure testbed development supporting research, education, and industry demonstrations. Current research explores Digital Twins, AI-driven threat intelligence, and autonomous cyber defence approaches for protecting essential systems against evolving cyber threats.

Advanced Manufacturing & Industry 5.0

My research investigates secure and resilient manufacturing systems by integrating Digital Twins, Artificial Intelligence, knowledge graphs, and cybersecurity engineering. Through the ECU EMCR project “Towards Resilient Factories: Digital Twins for Safeguarding Manufacturing Systems” and the Industry 5.0 Category 2/3 research award, I develop context-aware security models for smart factories. The research combines industrial automation, cyber-physical systems, threat intelligence, and AI reasoning to support trustworthy human-centric manufacturing environments and adaptive cyber protection.

Future Energy Systems & Trusted Digital Twins

I lead research addressing cybersecurity challenges in future energy infrastructure through the CSIRO–AEMO AR-PST Stage 5 project. The research develops a Trusted Sovereign Monitor architecture for securing Distributed Energy Resources and EV charging infrastructure. The project investigates trusted firmware validation, policy enforcement, cyber-physical coordination, and resilience mechanisms for future energy systems. This research contributes towards secure renewable energy transitions by combining cybersecurity, Digital Twins, and system resilience approaches for critical energy infrastructure protection.

Human–AI Collaboration & Cyber Defence

My research explores how humans and AI systems can collaboratively support cybersecurity operations through trusted autonomy. This work investigates AI-assisted Security Operations Centres, explainable decision support, autonomy levels, and human oversight mechanisms. The ACM Transactions on Internet Technology article develops a framework for Human–AI collaboration in SOC environments, while CyberAlly investigates the integration of Large Language Models and knowledge graphs to empower cyber defenders. These contributions address balancing automation, trust, accountability, and effective human decision-making in modern cyber defence.

Agentic AI & Secure Code Generation

My research investigates the security implications of Large Language Models used for software development and autonomous coding assistants. The “Can We Trust Large Language Models Generated Code?” framework examines how In-Context Learning, security patterns, and reasoning approaches can improve the reliability and security of AI-generated software. This research explores secure-by-design AI coding workflows, vulnerability reduction, code evaluation methods, and responsible adoption of agentic AI development tools in modern software engineering practices.

AI Safety, Governance & Trustworthy Systems

My research focuses on developing governance frameworks and safety mechanisms for responsible AI adoption in critical and high-impact environments. Current research explores AI governance for critical infrastructure, trustworthy AI decision-making, risk management, and secure deployment of intelligent systems. This work aligns AI development with safety, security, transparency, and accountability principles. Research activities include AI risk assessment, explainable AI, responsible LLM adoption, and governance models supporting organisations transitioning towards AI-enabled operations.
