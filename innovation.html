Innovation & R&D

Turning Research into Real-World Impact

Innovation transforms research knowledge into practical solutions that improve society, strengthen industry capability, and address complex national challenges. Successful innovation requires collaboration between universities, industry, government, and communities to translate ideas into technologies, products, and services.

Countries such as the United States have demonstrated the importance of sustained investment in research, industry collaboration, entrepreneurship, and technology development. Strong innovation ecosystems have enabled major advances in artificial intelligence, computing, aerospace, biotechnology, defence technologies, and advanced manufacturing.

For Australia, innovation is critical for strengthening sovereign capability, improving productivity, supporting advanced industries, enhancing cybersecurity resilience, and developing future technologies for Defence, energy, manufacturing, and critical infrastructure.

Responsible Innovation in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is transforming organisations, industries, and society. However, successful AI adoption requires more than developing powerful models. Organisations need AI systems that are safe, secure, reliable, transparent, and aligned with human values.

The rapid growth of Generative AI and autonomous AI agents creates significant opportunities while introducing challenges including AI reliability, security vulnerabilities, privacy risks, data governance, bias, explainability, and responsible decision-making.

My research focuses on trustworthy AI approaches that combine innovation with safety, security, governance, and human oversight. This includes AI security evaluation, secure Large Language Models, human–AI collaboration, AI-enabled cybersecurity, and responsible deployment of intelligent systems.

AI Safety, Security and Trustworthy AI

AI Safety

Developing approaches that ensure AI systems operate safely and predictably by reducing unintended consequences, improving reliability, and enabling appropriate human oversight.

AI Security

Addressing emerging AI threats including adversarial attacks, data manipulation, insecure AI deployment, prompt injection, model vulnerabilities, and security risks associated with AI agents.

Responsible AI

Supporting responsible AI adoption through transparency, accountability, fairness, privacy protection, ethical considerations, and alignment with organisational objectives.

Human–AI Collaboration

Designing intelligent systems where AI augments human expertise and supports decision-making in security-critical and complex operational environments.

Challenges Organisations Face in AI Adoption

Trust and Governance

Organisations require effective AI governance frameworks to understand risks, establish accountability, and build confidence in AI-enabled decisions.

Security and Privacy Risks

AI introduces new attack surfaces including data leakage, model manipulation, insecure interfaces, and vulnerabilities in AI-enabled workflows.

Data and Capability Gaps

Successful AI implementation requires quality data, skilled teams, appropriate infrastructure, and organisational capability.

Legacy System Integration

Many organisations face challenges integrating AI technologies with existing enterprise systems, operational technology, and cyber-physical environments.
