Innovation & R&D

Turning Research into Real-World Impact

I work with universities, industry, government, and R&D teams to turn research challenges into testable approaches and practical outcomes. My focus is on trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, digital twins, and resilient cyber-physical systems, particularly where secure adoption needs evidence from real operational settings.

If you are developing a new capability, evaluating an AI or security risk, or shaping a multi-partner innovation proposal, we can identify the research question, build a prototype or testbed, and define how to evaluate it together.

I welcome innovation partnerships, research consultation, co-designed pilots, and discussions about aligned senior research or innovation leadership roles. Tell me the challenge, expected users, collaborators, and the decision your evidence needs to support.
