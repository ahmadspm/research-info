Interpretation boundary

What this demo establishes

Browser exploration

Useful for teaching, rapid parameter exploration and inspecting the common sequential control policy.

Not native execution

It does not compile Modelica or Structured Text and does not replace FDR refinement checking or full SCXML conformance.

Illustrative plant

The plant is uncalibrated and is not a reconstruction of operational infrastructure. Results are not safety assurance.
