Supervision and researcher development

As described in my current teaching and research portfolio, I supervise six PhD candidates and one Master by Research candidate. I have recruited five funded HDR candidates and supported four PhD candidates through candidature confirmation. Earlier, I was principal supervisor for eight undergraduate Honours theses and three completed Master of Science (Research) theses at Air University.

I support research planning, proposal and methodology development, experimental design, publication writing, candidature milestones, and thesis preparation. Students can connect with interdisciplinary projects and research infrastructure where their topic and project arrangements align.

How we work together

  1. Shape the problem. Define the research gap, scope, and contribution in dialogue with the supervisory team.
  2. Choose rigorous methods. Match models, datasets, testbeds, evaluation measures, and ethics requirements to the question.
  3. Review milestones. Develop a practical plan for proposal, candidature progression, experiments, publications, and thesis writing.
  4. Share the evidence. Communicate reproducible findings through papers, demonstrations, or industry engagement as appropriate.
