About Dr Ahmad Mohsin

Biography

Dr Ahmad Mohsin is a systems researcher working at the intersection of Systems Engineering, Systems-of-Systems modelling, Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Digital Twins, and resilience.

Dr Mohsin is a Senior Researcher at the Centre for Securing Digital Futures (CSDF), Edith Cowan University, Australia, and collaborates with academic, industry and government partners in cybersecurity research.

Research areas

Systems Engineering and Systems-of-Systems Research

Dr Mohsin's research foundation is in Systems-of-Systems engineering, software architecture, stochastic modelling and simulation, and verification of complex adaptive systems.

Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructure Resilience

His cybersecurity research addresses the protection of critical infrastructure and operational technology environments, focusing on threat analytics, security-by-design, and resilience mechanisms.

Digital Twins, Industry 5.0 and Future Systems

Research includes secure Digital Twin architectures that combine simulation, semantic reasoning, AI, and cybersecurity analytics to improve understanding and resilience of complex systems.

Trustworthy AI and Human–AI Collaboration

Current research explores trustworthy AI, LLM safety and security, and human–AI collaboration for reliable and auditable AI systems.
