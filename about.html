About Dr Ahmad Mohsin

Dr Ahmad Mohsin is a systems researcher working at the intersection of Systems Engineering, Systems-of-Systems modelling, Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Digital Twins, and resilient cyber-physical systems. He has over 15 years of experience across academia and industry, focusing on the design, modelling, simulation, verification, and security assurance of complex software-intensive systems.

Dr Mohsin is a Senior Researcher at the Centre for Securing Digital Futures (CSDF), Edith Cowan University, Australia, and contributes to cybersecurity research through collaborations with research organisations and industry partners. He holds a PhD in Computer Science and Software Engineering from Edith Cowan University, with research focused on stochastic software architecture modelling and analysis for Systems-of-Systems. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Systems Engineering and Systems-of-Systems Research

Dr Mohsin's research foundation is in Systems-of-Systems engineering, software architecture, stochastic modelling and simulation, and verification of complex adaptive systems. His doctoral research investigated approaches for modelling and analysing dynamic Systems-of-Systems architectures where independent constituent systems interact, evolve, and influence overall system behaviour, reliability, and performance. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

His research explores how interconnected autonomous systems, software platforms, and physical assets can be architected, simulated, and assured to achieve dependable operation. This foundation supports his current research in cyber-physical systems, autonomous systems, Digital Twins, and resilient critical infrastructure.

Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructure Resilience

Dr Mohsin contributes to cybersecurity research addressing the protection of critical infrastructure and operational technology environments. Through the Augmenting Cyber Defense Capabilities (ACDC) project, he has contributed to cyber-physical architecture modelling, maritime infrastructure security, cyber incident response modelling, cyber wargaming, and threat analysis approaches aligned with industry frameworks.

His cybersecurity research investigates intelligent threat analytics, security-by-design approaches, knowledge-driven security models, and resilience mechanisms for complex digital ecosystems.

Digital Twins, Industry 5.0 and Future Systems

Dr Mohsin investigates secure Digital Twin architectures that combine simulation, semantic reasoning, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity analytics to improve understanding and resilience of complex systems. His research explores how Digital Twins can support continuous monitoring, threat intelligence, assurance, and secure decision-making across critical infrastructure and industrial environments. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

His recent research includes Industry 5.0 cybersecurity for advanced manufacturing systems and Trusted Sovereign Monitor approaches for resilient energy systems. These projects investigate how AI-enabled technologies can strengthen operational resilience, security, and sovereign capability in future infrastructure.

Trustworthy AI and Human–AI Collaboration

Dr Mohsin's current research explores trustworthy artificial intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), generative AI security, AI safety, and human–AI collaboration. His work investigates how emerging AI technologies can be safely integrated into operational environments while maintaining reliability, appropriate autonomy, and human oversight.

His research includes evaluating the security and reliability of LLM-generated code, developing AI-grounded cybersecurity knowledge systems, and designing trusted autonomy approaches for Security Operations Centres.
