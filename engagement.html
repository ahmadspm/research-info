Research Talks, Workshops & Engagement

Dr Ahmad Mohsin regularly delivers invited talks, seminars, workshops, and technical briefings for academic institutions, industry partners, government stakeholders, and professional communities. His engagement activities focus on trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, Digital Twins, Systems Engineering, Systems-of-Systems modelling, and resilient cyber-physical systems.

Through research communication and knowledge exchange, Dr Mohsin connects fundamental research with practical challenges in Defence, critical infrastructure, energy systems, advanced manufacturing, and secure digital transformation.
