BRIDG-ICS: AI-grounded Knowledge Graphs for Intelligent Threat Analytics in Industry 5.0 Cyber-Physical Systems

Padmeswari Nandiya, Ahmad Mohsin, Ahmed Ibrahim, Iqbal H. Sarker, Helge Janicke

Cybersecurity (2026). DOI 10.1186/s42400-026-00597-0

Research question

How can industrial defenders connect IT and operational technology data to reason about attack paths and compare mitigations?

Approach

The framework integrates industrial and cybersecurity data into a security knowledge graph. Domain-specific language models extract entities and relationships from threat descriptions, while graph analysis explores multi-hop attack paths and risk.

For researchers

Researchers studying industrial control system security, Industry 5.0, knowledge graphs, and cyber-physical resilience can use the published model and simulated scenarios to situate or compare threat analysis methods.
