A Unified Framework for Human–AI Collaboration in Security Operations Centers with Trusted Autonomy

Ahmad Mohsin, Helge Janicke, Ahmed Ibrahim, Iqbal H. Sarker, Seyit A. Camtepe

ACM Transactions on Internet Technology (2026). DOI 10.1145/3837073

Research question

How should security operations centers allocate monitoring, triage, and response tasks between analysts and AI assistants?

Approach

The article maps five levels of AI autonomy to human roles and task-specific trust thresholds. A simulated cyber range and AI assistant illustrate the framework in security operations.

For researchers

Researchers studying SOC automation, human-in-the-loop decision-making, trust calibration, and accountable AI can use the autonomy framework and case study as a starting point.
