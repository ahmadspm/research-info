Research Focus Areas

My research develops trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, and Digital Twin solutions for resilient cyber-physical systems, critical infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, and future energy systems. Research questions include how to calibrate human oversight in AI-assisted security operations, trace multi-stage attacks through industrial systems, and govern connected energy assets under cyber risk. Each area below links to a relevant publication or project resource.

Critical Infrastructure Resilience

I investigate how connected IT and operational technology can withstand cyber incidents in critical infrastructure. Work with ECU’s ACDC program and cyber-physical testbeds informs threat modelling, cyber range scenarios, and resilience evaluation.

CyberAlly: LLMs and Knowledge Graphs
Explainable AI for Cybersecurity Digital Twins

Research question: How can operators assess cascading cyber risk across connected IT and OT assets?

Approach: Model assets, attack paths, and operational effects with knowledge graphs and cyber-physical testbeds.

Explore the evidence: BRIDG-ICS threat analytics

Advanced Manufacturing & Industry 5.0

I develop security models for smart factories using digital twins, knowledge graphs, and industrial system data. The ECU resilient factories projects and BRIDG-ICS examine how defenders can trace multi-stage attack paths and compare mitigations.

BRIDG-ICS: AI-grounded Knowledge Graphs
Security Risks and Privacy Challenges in Digital Twins

Research question: How can a factory detect and explain threats in its changing physical and digital state?

Approach: Connect digital twin state, industrial assets, and graph-based threat reasoning.

Explore the evidence: HySecTwin research details

Future Energy Systems & Trusted Digital Twins

The CSIRO–AEMO AR-PST Stage 5 project investigates trusted firmware, policy enforcement, and cyber-physical resilience for EV charging and distributed energy resources. A Trusted Sovereign Monitor and simulation environment support evaluation of these mechanisms.

CSIRO AR-PST Program
AR-PST STAGE 5- Topic 9-2 (DERs, EV infrastructure and GRIDS Cyber Reslience
Policy White Paper: Secure EV Charging — Cyber Risk, Grid Stability and Sovereign Assurance

Research question: How can EV charging and distributed energy resources retain trusted operation under cyber risk?

Approach: Study policy enforcement, firmware trust, and grid-aware simulation through the Trusted Sovereign Monitor project.

Explore the evidence: TSM policy white paper

Human–AI Collaboration & Cyber Defence

I study how analysts and AI assistants can share security operations tasks under clear human oversight. The trusted autonomy framework defines task-level authority and trust thresholds; CyberAlly explores LLMs and knowledge graphs as analyst support.

Human–AI Collaboration in SOCs
CyberAlly

Research question: Which security tasks should AI support, and when must analysts retain decision authority?

Approach: Evaluate task-specific autonomy, human oversight, and evidence in security operations.

Explore the evidence: Human–AI SOC framework

Agentic AI & Secure Code Generation

This work examines the security of LLM-generated code and AI-assisted software development. It evaluates security patterns, context, and testing methods for more reliable coding workflows.

Can We Trust Large Language Models Generated Code?

Research question: How can teams evaluate the security of code produced with LLM assistance?

Approach: Examine in-context security guidance, generated-code evaluation, and human review.

Explore the evidence: Secure LLM-generated code paper

AI Safety, Governance & Trustworthy Systems

I examine how organizations can govern AI risks in critical and high-impact settings. Research addresses accountability, explainability, risk assessment, and responsible adoption of AI and LLMs.

Explainable AI for Trustworthy Cybersecurity

Research question: How can organizations adopt AI while preserving trust, accountability, and human control?

Approach: Study security and ethics requirements, explainability, and governance of AI-assisted work.
