How we work together

  1. Shape the problem. Define the research gap, scope, and contribution in dialogue with the supervisory team.
  2. Choose rigorous methods. Match models, datasets, testbeds, evaluation measures, and ethics requirements to the question.
  3. Review milestones. Develop a practical plan for proposal, candidature progression, experiments, publications, and thesis writing.
  4. Share the evidence. Communicate reproducible findings through papers, demonstrations, or industry engagement as appropriate.
