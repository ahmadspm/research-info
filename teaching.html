Teaching and curriculum

Academic Teaching

I connect computer science foundations with real problems in software engineering, cybersecurity, AI, and digital twins. Students learn to explain design choices, build and assess systems, and communicate the evidence behind their decisions.

Teaching approach

Research-informed learning

Bring current work in trustworthy AI, cyber-physical systems, and secure software into lectures and projects, while building from established computing principles.

Authentic assessment

Use case studies, collaborative design tasks, labs, and project artefacts to connect learning outcomes with professional practice and clear feedback.

Inclusive, flexible delivery

Support different learning pathways through on-campus, blended, and online teaching, formative feedback, and opportunities to question assumptions.
