Teaching and curriculum

Academic Teaching

I bring more than 15 years of teaching and academic leadership experience across universities in Australia and Pakistan. My undergraduate and Master's teaching connects computer science foundations to software engineering, cybersecurity, AI, and real research and industry problems.

Teaching excellence across national and international settings

My experience includes teaching and unit coordination at Edith Cowan University, project-based teaching contributions at the University of Western Australia, teaching and academic leadership at Air University, and large-scale online teaching at the Virtual University of Pakistan. I have taught on campus, online, and in blended formats, adapting learning activities to diverse student cohorts. Air University recognized me as Best Teacher among its Computer Science faculty in 2017.

Undergraduate teaching

Software architecture, object-oriented analysis and design, software engineering, operating systems, and systems analysis, with lectures, labs, tutorials, design tasks, and capstone mentoring.

Master's teaching

Postgraduate systems analysis and database design, software engineering, research methods mentoring, proposal development, and research project guidance.

Student-centered practice

Clear outcomes and rubrics, inclusive teaching, formative feedback, collaborative problem solving, and authentic project assessment support learning and progression.

Teaching approach

Research-informed learning

Bring current work in trustworthy AI, cyber-physical systems, and secure software into lectures and projects, while building from established computing principles.

Authentic assessment

Use case studies, collaborative design tasks, labs, and project artefacts to connect learning outcomes with professional practice and clear feedback.

Inclusive, flexible delivery

Support different learning pathways through on-campus, blended, and online teaching, formative feedback, and opportunities to question assumptions.

Unit coordination, degree advising, and curriculum renewal

At ECU I coordinated CSI306 Software Architecture and Design and CSI6207 Systems Analysis and Database Design, including unit planning, teaching teams, learning resources, assessment design, moderation, and student consultation. At Air University I served as program advisor and coordinator for the BS Computer Science degree across three graduating cohorts.

My curriculum development work includes updating unit outcomes, assessment rubrics, and project activities at ECU and designing postgraduate computer science and cybersecurity curricula as a subject matter expert at Stanley College. I connect this work to advances in secure software engineering and cyber defence, including the responsible use and evaluation of generative AI, large language models (LLMs), and emerging AI agents. These topics inform lectures and new learning activities while core design and security principles remain explicit.
